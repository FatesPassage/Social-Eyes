# Social Eyes — Brand System

## approved visual direction
Social Eyes uses a midnight-blue foundation with cyan, electric blue, violet, and restrained pink highlights. The approved brand line is **Discover. Connect. Belong.** This is a privacy-first, welcoming real-world discovery product, not a surveillance or tracking experience.

## Brand identity
The primary icon is the Social Eyes eye mark. Preserve the icon's silhouette and approved visual identity across native launch icons, adaptive foregrounds, welcome screens, and headers. Do not silently replace approved brand artwork with a generic placeholder.

## Typography and layout
Use strong, readable headings; compact supporting copy; ample touch targets; and responsive vertical spacing. The welcome page prioritizes the brand mark, a concise value proposition, clear opt-in privacy cues, and one primary action.

## Color palette
- Midnight: `#080B1E`
- Deep navy: `#0A1030`
- Cyan: `#2CE6F5`
- Electric blue: `#348EFF`
- Violet: `#8C55F6`
- Pink: `#EF6BBE`
- White: `#F8FAFF`

## Motion
Keep transitions purposeful and respect reduced-motion preferences. Brand reveal target: 350 ms; iris focus: 450 ms; wordmark fade: 250 ms. Never block access to the app on a decorative animation.

## Safety
Opt-in presence and mutual connections must remain explicit. Decorative radar imagery must not imply precise location tracking. Brand artwork and application copy must never suggest that a person is visible without consent.

## Verification
Asset sizes, alpha channels, launch icon dimensions, device screenshots, and reduced-motion behavior require independent testing. This document does not constitute visual or native verification.
